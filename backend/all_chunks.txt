ABSTRACT The development of agentic software engineering (ASE) is shifting the software development process, specifically by adopting autonomous agents into the fundamental software development process, where autonomous agents using large language models (LLMs) and artificial intelligence (AI) make decisions, write code, and collaborate across the entire software development lifecycle. The systematic review is a summary of literature on the agentic software engineering methodologies, frameworks, and techniques published in 2022-2025. We identify and compare methods based on autonomous coding, multi- agent systems, iterative refinement, and human-agent collaboration, drawing on 61 high-quality studies

================================================================================
CHUNK ID: 2
================================================================================
. We find that LLM-based agents show substantial potential in automated software engineering and that recent work is exploring new trends in agent cooperation, reasoning systems, and tool integration. We recognize such key challenges as the coordination procedures, reliability, explainability, and the ethical issues. This review characterizes the emerging role of agentic software engineering in improving the productivity of developers, the quality of their code, and the automation of their processes, and it offers a structured roadmap for future research. With the rapid evolution of the field, agentic approaches will tend to transform the conception, creation, and maintenance of software and provide more autonomous, adaptive, and intelligent systems

RELATIONSHIP TO EXISTING PARADIGMS Agentic software engineering builds on several established paradigms while adding new dimensions of autonomy, intelligence, and collaboration

================================================================================
CHUNK ID: 9
================================================================================
. In the framework of multi- agent systems, classical methods have considered distributed problem solving by communication and coordination of computational objects. The same concept is extended by agentic software engineering, which adds advanced reasoning and natural language understanding to agents that are specifically created to accomplish software development tasks [1], [11], [12]. This evolution is a major divergence of the traditional multi-agent architectures, as it makes domain specific knowledge and development processes part of the agent design. The field has also been the continuation of decades of work to apply AI-driven development, which has long been using machine learning to assist specialised tasks such as code completion, bug prediction, or test generation

================================================================================
CHUNK ID: 10
================================================================================
. However, agentic approaches can be characterized by the fact that they generalize this functionality to allow end-to- end autonomous systems able to finish workflows and make high-level architectural choices [3], [5], [13]. Such systems can orchestrate the entire development activities, such as requirements analysis, deployment and maintenance, instead of providing discrete support to individual development activities. Moreover, prior work on autonomous software systems also motivates agentic software engineering. Traditionally, autonomy has referred to the ability of software systems to self-adapt and manage themselves at runtime

================================================================================
CHUNK ID: 11
================================================================================
. ASE extends these ideas to the development process itself, introducing agents that can support and partially automate software construction and evolution [6], [10], [14]. The paradigm shift moves the autonomy as an operation aspect to self-creation and alteration of the systems. B. DRIVERS FOR THE AGENTIC SHIFT Several interconnected factors are precipitating this large-scale change in software engineering. The exponential increase of software complexity is the most obvious driver as modern software systems have developed into highly complex dependency networks, mixed technological layers, and large volumes of code that transcend human cognitive limits

================================================================================
CHUNK ID: 12
================================================================================
. This complexity introduces an excessive cognitive load that can be reduced in autonomous agents by advanced strategies of automated reasoning and intelligent task decomposing mechanisms [13], [15], [16], [17]. Modern software projects are often of such a scale that they can be handled only by intelligent help as human teams often lack the managerial capacity to address them. At the same time, the unparalleled magnitude and rate of contemporary software development have led to the need of more independent treatment of routine and repetitive duties. It is becoming more apparent within organizations that human developers need to direct their skills to high-level architectural issues and strategic business needs and not on the low-level details of implementation [17]

================================================================================
CHUNK ID: 13
================================================================================
. This change in attitude has led to high demand of smart automation that can be used in dealing with the routine development activities without compromising human ingenuity and determination on the most important part of software designing. This transformation has been enabled by recent advances in natural language processing and machine learning. In particular, the advent of large transformer-based language models has made advanced natural-language understanding and code generation practically feasible [4], [5], [18]. Such developments have enabled the development of agents capable of comprehending sophisticated specifications that can be expressed in natural language and then converted directly into functional implementations of codes

================================================================================
CHUNK ID: 14
================================================================================
. Finally, competition and the ongoing talent crunch of the software sector have exacerbated corporate requirements to employ tools and strategies that may exponentially increase developer velocity and shorten software develop- ment cycles [3], [19]. Businesses are becoming more and more interested in agentic practices not only as a fascinating line of research but also as important competitive advantages in highly dynamic markets where the speed and quality of software delivery can spell the difference between business success and failure.
